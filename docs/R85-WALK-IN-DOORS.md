## Tests

  walking out returns to that door (re-arm); VIEW sign enters, WALK sign guides (compass); camera second difference
  < 20 mm; life census, seasons key, the south park is walkable.
- `tests/r70.spec.ts`: signs in walk mode now guide (beacon + compass), never teleport. Its 180 Hz smoothness walk starts at the spawn
  and heads north into the konbini doorway, so it switches walking-in off with `debug.walkIn(false)` (the doors still
  open) - the measurement and its limits are unchanged.
- `tests/tokyo-diorama.spec.ts`: the konbini's doors are now the way into Projects, so the wall check moved beside them.
